---
title: "Button Group"
description: "Button groups are designed to bring together button controls that are of a similar nature. For example text formatting controls."
components: [cat-button-group]
---

```html preview
<cat-button-group>
  <cat-button button-group-position="first">Left</cat-button>
  <cat-button button-group-position="last">Right</cat-button>
</cat-button-group>
```

## Examples

### Grouped actions

```html preview
<cat-button-group>
  <cat-button button-group-position="first">Left</cat-button>
  <cat-button button-group-position="middle">Center</cat-button>
  <cat-button button-group-position="last">Right</cat-button>
</cat-button-group>
```
